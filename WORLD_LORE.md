# Complete RPG World Lore

## Core World Concepts

### The Awakening System

**80% of the population experiences "The Awakening" between ages 8-16**
- Grants access to the System Interface
- Unlocks class potential and stat progression
- Determines lifetime power ceiling

**20% remain "Mundane"**
- No system access
- Become craftsmen, scholars, support roles
- Rare exception: .001% born with natural {Mana Control}

**Universal Truth: The younger you awaken, the stronger your potential becomes**

#### Age Brackets

- **Ages 8-10: "Prodigies"** - Extremely rare, become legendary figures
- **Ages 11-13: "Early Blessed"** - Strong foundation, often nobility/adventurer material
- **Ages 14-16: "Standard Awakened"** - Most common, average potential
- **Ages 17+: "Late Bloomers"** - Weaker base stats but develop unique insights

#### Historical Context

- **First recorded awakening:** 1,000 years ago (Year 0)
- **Before awakening:** Power came from training, bloodlines, and artifacts
- **Interface:** Appears as translucent panels with different visual styles based on class affinity

## Racial Information

### Elves - The First Awakened

**Lifespan:** 800-1,200 years

**Awakening Rate:** 95%, ages 25-40 (equivalent to human 8-12)

**Culture:** Awakening seen as "returning to ancestral wisdom"

**Interface Style:** Elegant, nature-themed with flowing script

**Unique Traits:**
- Can sense other awakened within 50 meters
- +20% Mana Regeneration
- Natural Magic Resistance

**Society:** Ruled by council of earliest awakened (some over 900 years old)

---

### Orcs - The Reluctant Awakened

**Lifespan:** 60-80 years

**Awakening Rate:** 60%, ages 6-10 (mature faster)

**Culture:** View system as "spirit talking," prefer traditional strength

**Interface Style:** Crude, runic stone tablets

**Unique Traits:**
- Explosive raw stat growth
- +30% Physical Damage
- -10% Magic Affinity

**Society:** Chieftains often non-awakened who proved themselves through pure strength

---

### Dwarves - The Master Craftsmen

**Lifespan:** 150-200 years

**Awakening Rate:** 70%, ages 12-18

**Culture:** Value precision and engineering excellence

**Interface Style:** Geometric blueprints and measurements

**Unique Traits:**
- Natural affinity for crafting and alchemy
- +25% Crafting Speed
- Natural Engineering Aptitude

**Society:** Ruled by master engineers and craft guild leaders

---

### Humans - The Adaptable

**Lifespan:** 60-80 years

**Awakening Rate:** 82%, ages 8-16

**Culture:** Most diverse, accepting of all paths

**Interface Style:** Varies wildly by individual

**Unique Traits:**
- Can learn techniques from ANY race
- +15% Experience Gain
- Racial Skill Learning (can learn any race's techniques)

**Society:** Democratic assemblies with multi-racial representation

---

### Dragon-kin - The Hunted Survivors

**Lifespan:** 2,000+ years (if they survive)

**Awakening Rate:** 99%, but only at age 100+ (their adolescence)

**Culture:** Consider awakening a "birthright returning"

**Interface Style:** Ancient draconic glyphs wreathed in flame

**Unique Traits:**
- Can share interface functions with "hoard-bond" (chosen family/allies)
- Perfect Racial Mimicry - can perfectly replicate non-dragon parent's race
- Dragon Form - can transform into inferior but still powerful dragon shape
- No Pride Markers - cannot manifest draconic eyes or scales

**Society:** NO KINGDOM - hunted survivors living in hiding

**Survival Status:**
- Population: 50-100 adult survivors worldwide
- Birth Rate: 20-30 born per year across all continents
- Survival Rate: Less than 1% survive to adulthood
- Life Expectancy: Most die by age 10 if they survive initial culling

**Universal Execution Protocol:**
- Birth Detection: Magical/technological means identify dragonkin births immediately
- Universal Law: ALL races participate in genocide - no exceptions
- Mother's Fate: Any female who births dragonkin is executed
- Immediate Action: Killing happens within hours/days of birth
- Cross-Border Cooperation: Assassins can cross borders freely for dragonkin hunting

---

### Furries - The Pack Awakened

**Lifespan:** 40-60 years

**Awakening Rate:** 85%, ages 3-8 (earliest of all races)

**Culture:** Awakening ceremonies are massive pack celebrations

**Interface Style:** Territorial markings and scent-trails

**Unique Traits:**
- Pack members can form "interface clusters" - sharing buffs and information
- Enhanced Senses
- Pack Coordination Bonuses

**Society:** Led by "Alpha Awakened" - earliest awakened in each pack

---

### True Dragons - The Ancient Powers

**Lifespan:** Immortal (unless killed)

**Awakening Rate:** 100%, at hatching

**Culture:** Supremely proud, consider all other races inferior

**Interface Style:** Reality-warping cosmic script

**Unique Traits:**
- Perfect shapeshifting while maintaining pride markers (draconic eyes, neck/arm scales)
- Reality-level shapeshifting
- Parental Instinct (know when they have offspring and if alive, but not location)
- Immense Power Suppression

**Society:** NO FORMAL KINGDOM - infiltrate all governments in disguise

**Infiltration Network:**
- Hold high offices in every kingdom
- Policy Influence: Ensure dragonkin hunting remains legal and funded
- Bounty System: Secretly fund the hunting of their own dragonkin children
- Global Coordination: Work across all kingdoms to maintain reputation
- Reputation Protection: Eliminate evidence of "breeding with lesser beings"

## Interface System

### The Five Main Tabs

#### 1. STATS Tab

**Physical Details:**
- Height, weight, hair length/color, eye color
- Scars, tattoos, dental health
- Extremely detailed body measurements

**Combat Stats:**
- Health, mana/aura, strength, agility, intelligence
- Class-specific stats (mana for mages, aura for warriors, void essence for void classes)

**Hidden Stats:**
- Pain tolerance, fear resistance, social charisma, luck coefficient

**Achievements & Titles:**
- Achievements: "Dragon Slayer," "Master Craftsman," "Political Manipulator"
- Active Titles: Can equip 1-3 titles providing stat bonuses or special abilities
- Status Effects: Curses, blessings, diseases, temporary buffs/debuffs

#### 2. SKILLS Tab

**Structure:** Main Skills → Sub-Skills → Masteries → Class Rankings

**Main Skills:** Broad categories (Hand-to-Hand Combat, Elemental Magic, Stealth Arts)

**Sub-Skills:** Specific techniques directly connected to main skills
- Example: Dragon-Slaying Technique (sub-skill of Hand-to-Hand Combat)

**Mastery Levels:**
```
[Newbie] → [Beginner] → [Intermediate] → [Advanced] → [Expert] →
[Master] → [Grandmaster] → [Transcendent] → [Mythical]
```

**Class Rankings:**
```
F-- → F- → F → F+ → F++ →
E-- → E- → E → E+ → E++ →
D-- → D- → D → D+ → D++ →
C-- → C- → C → C+ → C++ →
B-- → B- → B → B+ → B++ →
A-- → A- → A → A+ → A++ →
S-- → S- → S → S+ → S++ →
SS-- → SS- → SS → SS+ → SS++ →
SSS-- → SSS- → SSS → SSS+ → SSS++ →
EX-- → EX- → EX → EX+ → EX++ →
EXX-- → EXX- → EXX → EXX+ → EXX++
```

#### 3. STORE Tab

- **Continental Marketplace:** Magically connected to every major trading hub
- **Dynamic Pricing:** Supply/demand affects costs in real-time
- **Reputation System:** Better deals for higher merchant standing
- **Auction House:** Rare items bid on by players across continent
- **Black Market:** Hidden section for illegal/rare items (requires special access)

#### 4. INVENTORY Tab

- **Weight/Space Limits:** Based on strength and bag quality
- **Categories:** Weapons, armor, consumables, materials, quest items
- **Quick-Access Slots:** Combat items for instant use
- **Currency Tracking:** Gold, regional coins, barter credits
- **Spell Scrolls:** Learned spells and techniques stored here

#### 5. PARTY Tab

- **Relationship Status:** Ally/Enemy/Neutral with trust percentages
- **Location Tracking:** See party members on mini-map if close enough
- **Shared Quests:** Group objectives and progress
- **Communication:** Send messages across distances to party members

## Skill Evolution System

### Basic Mechanics

- All skills start at [Newbie] mastery regardless of rank
- Experience points and levels determine skill progression
- **Skill rank** determines raw power/strength
- **Versatility** determines mastery ceiling potential

### Evolution Types

**Minor Evolution (Rank Modifier Change):**
- Rock Throw (F--) → Rock Throw | Flurry Variant (F)
- Same core skill, improved execution

**Major Evolution (Rank Letter Jump):**
- Basic Sword Style (D-) → Advanced Sword Style (C)
- Skill fundamentally transforms due to versatility/training

### Mastery Ceiling Examples

- **Speed Boost** (E-Rank, Low Versatility): Caps at [Advanced] mastery
- **Pyromancy** (A-Rank, High Versatility): Caps at [Grandmaster] mastery
- **Divine Smite** (S-Rank, Low Versatility): Caps at [Expert] mastery
- **Hand-to-Hand Combat** (C-Rank, Extreme Versatility): Caps at [Mythical] mastery

### Evolution Factors

- **Training Time:** More practice = better evolution potential
- **Versatility Discovery:** Finding new applications during training
- **Combat Experience:** Real-world usage unlocks hidden potential
- **Mastery Understanding:** Deep comprehension can cause rank jumps

## Magic System

### Magic Prerequisites

**Mana Control Requirement:**

**Awakened (80%):** Can learn {Mana Control} skill through system interface

**Mundane (20%):** Normally have NO access to mana

**Rare Mundane (.001% of mundanes):** Born with natural {Mana Control}
- No interface access
- Learn through pure instinct and experimentation
- Often develop unconventional techniques
- **Out of 100,000 people: only 2 naturally magical mundane**

### Magic Classifications

#### Elemental Magic

- **Fire Magic:** Fire tornadoes, lava manipulation, burning forests, melting steel
- **Water Magic:** Freeze lakes, create tsunamis, control rainstorms
- **Earth Magic:** Cause earthquakes, raise stone walls, create mountain paths
- **Air Magic:** Generate hurricanes, create vacuums, fly at high speeds
- **Lightning Magic:** Lightning storms, electromagnetic pulses, chain lightning

#### Arcane Magic

- **Pure Magic:** Magic projectiles, barriers, mana manipulation
- **Illusion Magic:** Visual tricks, minor invisibility, sound mimicry
- **Enchantment Magic:** Temporary weapon enhancement, basic item improvement
- **Transmutation Magic:** Small material changes, basic alchemy

#### Divine Magic

- **Holy Magic:** Wound healing, minor blessings, basic purification
- **Dark Magic:** Minor curses, shadow control, basic necromancy
- **Nature Magic:** Plant growth assistance, animal empathy, weather sensing

#### Forbidden Magic

- **Void Magic:** Small reality tears, minor dimensional pockets, limited teleportation
- **Blood Magic:** Life force manipulation, enhanced healing, temporary strength boosts
- **Soul Magic:** Spirit communication, soul strengthening, minor soul healing

### Magic Limitations

**What Magic CANNOT Do:**

- ❌ No time manipulation (can't go back in time, stop time, age things instantly)
- ❌ No black holes/space warping (can't create gravitational singularities)
- ❌ No reality rewriting (can't change fundamental physics laws)
- ❌ No true resurrection (can't bring back permanently dead)
- ❌ No omniscience (can't know everything or see all futures)

**Realistic Constraints:**

- Mana exhaustion causes physical fatigue
- Complex magic can backfire
- Environmental limitations affect effectiveness
- Higher spells need material components/focuses

## Character Progression System

### Experience and Levels

- Traditional EXP system with level progression
- **5 stat points per level** (universal)
- Level determines rank advancement

### Stat Point Scaling

**Percentage Increase Varies By:**
- Individual rank
- Current level
- Personal talent factors
- Training methods
- Racial heritage
- Life experiences

**Scaling Examples:**

- E-Rank Awakened: ~23% increase per stat point
- EXX-Rank Awakened: ~5,870% increase per stat point
- F-Rank Level 500: ~500% per stat point (high level compensates)
- A-Rank Level 4: ~58% per stat point (high rank, very low level)

**Individual Variation:**

- Even same rank/level can have different percentages
- Natural talent affects scaling rates
- Major life events can permanently alter scaling

### Character Classes & Stats

**Mage Classes:**
- Mana, Spell Efficiency, Elemental Affinity

**Warrior Classes:**
- Aura, Physical Resistance, Combat Stance Mastery

**Void Classes:**
- Void Essence, Reality Distortion, Shadow Step Cooldown

**Beast Classes:**
- Transformation Energy, Animal Bond Strength, Primal Instinct

## Class System

### Class Rarity Tiers

```
[Normal] → [Uncommon] → [Rare] → [Special] → [Unique] →
[Epic] → [Legendary] → [Mythic] → [Godly]
```

### Class Acquisition Methods

#### Taught Classes

**Normal/Uncommon Tiers:** Can be learned from instructors, guilds, academies
- Examples: Warrior (military academies), Mage (magic schools), Archer (hunter guilds)

#### Quest-Obtained Classes

**Rare/Special Tiers:** Require completing specific questlines or trials
- Examples: Paladin (holy trials), Void Walker (survive void exposure), Assassin (shadow guild)

#### Special Case Classes

**Unique+ Tiers:** Extremely rare circumstances, once-in-a-lifetime events
- **Dragon Knight:** Save/bond with an actual dragon
- **Lich:** Survive a specific necromantic ritual
- **Phoenix Reborn:** Literally die and resurrect under special conditions
- **God Slayer:** Actually kill a divine being

#### Achievement-Based Classes

**Epic+ Tiers:** Unlocked through legendary accomplishments
- **World Guardian:** Successfully defend a continent from major threat
- **Ascended:** Perform feats that attract divine attention

#### Inherited Classes - The Ultimate Risk/Reward

**Source:** Only [Mythic] or [Godly] beings can grant inheritance

**Rarity:** 1-3 inheritances per millennia (1000 years)

**Power:** Always [Mythic] or [Godly] tier minimum

**Weakness:** Extremely weak start but world-dominating potential

**The Curse:** If recipient dies, killer can claim the inherited class

**Examples:**
- **Devourer:** Can absorb defeated enemies' classes and skills
- **God's Descendant:** Summon/control all angels, impose Divine Authority
- **World Eater:** Consume reality pieces to grow stronger
- **Fate Sovereign:** Rewrite destinies and manipulate probability
- **Death's Heir:** Control life/death, collect souls, master undead

### Class Functionality & Power Hierarchy

**Power Scaling Formula:**

- **Within Same Tier:** Awakening Rank determines dominance
- **Cross-Tier Dominance:** Higher class tiers override awakening rank differences
- **Mythic Override:** F-rank [Mythic] class > any [Legendary] or lower

**Class Benefits:**

- Specialized Skills: Unique skill trees only accessible to that class
- Stat Bonuses: Bonus points to specific stats per level
- Passive Abilities: Always-active benefits
- Class Evolution: Higher ranks unlock advanced versions

## The Great Kingdoms

### Elvish Dominions - "The Eternal Courts"

**Kingdom Structure:**
- **The Silverleaf Dominion:** Northern forests, ruled by Council of First Awakened
- **The Starwood Empire:** Eastern magical forests, focus on arcane research
- **The Moonhaven Federation:** Coastal elven cities, maritime traders

**Government:**
- Council of Ancients: Elves who awakened 800+ years ago
- Eternal Senators: Those awakened 400+ years ago

- Long-term Planning: Make decisions thinking centuries ahead
- Knowledge Preservation: Massive libraries spanning millennia
- Magical Innovation: Constantly developing new magical techniques
- Magitech Cooperation: Work with Orc-Dwarf alliance on magical enhancements

---

**Kingdom Structure:**
- **The United Clanholds:** Joint Orc-Dwarf territories in mountain ranges
- **The Great Forges:** Industrial cities where both races work together
- **The Innovation Republic:** Research centers and engineering marvels

**Scientific Achievements:**
- Mechanical Engineering: Steam-powered machinery, complex clockwork devices
- Metallurgy: Advanced alloys, precision smithing techniques
- Architecture: Massive underground cities with intricate ventilation systems
- Alchemy: Scientific approach to potion-making and material transformation

**Government:**
- The Twin Council: Equal representation from Orc clans and Dwarf holds
- Master Engineers: Technical experts who guide policy decisions
- Guild Cooperation: Craft guilds spanning both races working together

**Cultural Bond:**
- "Brothers by Choice": Adopted kinship stronger than blood relations
- Shared Values: Both races value craftsmanship, innovation, and practical strength
- Mutual Respect: Orcs appreciate dwarven precision, dwarves respect orcish innovation
- Combined Workshops: Mixed-race engineering teams create incredible inventions

## Historical Timeline

### Pre-Awakening Era (Before Year 0)

- Dragon-kin ruled through pure bloodline power and ancient magic
- Elves were skilled mages but limited in scope
- Orcs were nomadic tribes focused on physical strength
- Furries lived in small, scattered packs

### Year 0: The First Awakening

- **The Great Change:** First recorded awakening occurs
- **Initial Chaos:** Existing power structures collapse
- **The Awakening Wars:** 50-year period of global conflict as awakened beings reshape the world

### Years 50-500: The Stabilization Period

- **New Kingdoms Form:** Current political structures established
- **The First Inheritance:** Year 127 - Hidden dragon-kin receives [Godly] class "World Shaper"
- **Trade Networks:** Awakened individuals enable long-distance communication and trade

### Years 500-1000: The Golden Age

- **The Second Inheritance:** Year 742 - Elven noble becomes "Eternal Sage"
- **Great Alliance:** All four races unite against the Void Invasion (Year 834-847)
- **The Void Wars:** Mysterious entities from outside reality attempt conquest
- **Orc-Dwarf Engineering Corps:** Provided mechanical weapons during alliance

### Years 1000-1500: The Time of Troubles

- **The Third Inheritance:** Year 1205 - Dragon-kin becomes "Death's Champion"
- **The Century of Skulls:** Inherited class bearer causes continent-wide devastation
- **The Great Purge:** Combined effort to stop Death's Champion
- **Dragon-kin Persecution:** Led to systematic hunting

### Years 1500-2000: The Age of Expansion

- **New Continents:** Exploration beyond traditional lands
- **The Fourth Inheritance:** Year 1678 - Dragon-kin princess becomes "Star Caller"
- **Magic Revolution:** Awakening system understanding leads to magical technological advances
- **Magitech Development:** Beginning of technology-magic integration

### Years 2000-2500: The Modern Conflicts

- **The Fifth Inheritance:** Year 2134 - Furry pack leader becomes "Time Keeper"
- **Resource Wars:** Competition for awakening-enhancement materials
- **The Great Plague:** Year 2287-2295, disease killing millions of awakened
- **Human Commonwealth Formation:** Establishment of open-border policies

### Years 2500-3000 (Current Era): The Uncertain Times

- **The Sixth Inheritance:** Year 2756 - Human merchant becomes "Fate Weaver" (whereabouts unknown)
- **Rising Tensions:** All kingdoms suspicious of hidden inheritance bearer
- **The Mundane Rebellion:** Year 2934-2941, non-awakened attempt overthrow
- **Dragon-kin Genocide Formalization:** Year 2945 - Universal execution protocols adopted
- **Current Year 2987:** Uneasy peace, rumors of seventh inheritance approaching

## Progression Dynamics

### Early Game

- Rank matters most (A-rank dominates E-rank)
- Awakening age determines initial potential
- Skill selection crucial for long-term viability

### Mid Game

- Balance between rank and level
- Skill evolution becomes major power factor
- Mastery progression starts differentiating players

### Late Game

- Level starts mattering more
- High-level low-ranks become competitive
- Mastery caps determine ultimate potential
- Individual scaling discovery becomes crucial

### Strategic Considerations

**Immediate Power vs Long-term Investment:**
- High-rank skills for quick strength
- High-versatility skills become powerful at max mastery
- Balanced approach mixing both types
- Young awakening provides more time to maximize versatile skills
